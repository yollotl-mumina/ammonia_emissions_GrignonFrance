# Figure captions

**Figure 1.** NH₃-N emissions by cropping system, stacked by product category (EMEP Tier 2, full inventory 2018–2025).

**Figure 2.** NH₃-N emission intensity (percentage of applied nitrogen volatilized) by cropping system. The dashed line is the nitrogen-weighted mean of the whole set.

**Figure 3.** Indirect N₂O before (R1) and after (R2) the transition for the two changing lineages (CTR→COM, MF→LOM). The absolute total falls slightly while the intensity per kilogram of applied nitrogen rises (+21 %, +49 %). Error bars show the EF4 range.

**Figure 4.** EMEP versus ALFAM2 for the liquid organic events, with the EMEP band-spreading abatement applied to band events. Totals agree (ratio ≈ 1.0, versus 0.82 unabated); the distribution across methods still differs.

**Figure 5.** ALFAM2 cumulative emission (percentage of applied TAN) over time. Broadcast events reach high losses within hours; band (trailing-hose) events emit less and more gradually.

**Figure 6.** Real ALFAM2 re-run of the broadcast digestate events as band-applied under their actual July weather. Band application avoids about 30 kg NH₃-N ha⁻¹ (≈ 130 kg CO₂-eq ha⁻¹).

**Figure 7.** One-at-a-time sensitivity of the total inventory to each product's emission factor (±30 %). The UAN-solution factor dominates the response.

**Figure 8.** Comparison of the two rotations. The absolute total falls only because the second rotation covers a shorter record with less applied nitrogen; once normalized per year or per unit of applied nitrogen, emissions are equal or higher in the second rotation.

**Figure S1 (supplementary).** Effect of the Entec reclassification (ammonium sulphate → ammonium sulphate-nitrate) on the GHG system.
